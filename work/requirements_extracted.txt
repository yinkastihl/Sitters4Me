P7	Heading 1	1. Application Overview
P8	(no style)	Sitters4Me is a real-time on-demand babysitting marketplace modelled on the Uber dispatch model. Parents can instantly request a nearby sitter or schedule one in advance. Sitters receive push-notified job offers, accept or decline in 60 seconds, and are automatically paid via Stripe when the job ends. An admin portal at sitters4me.com/admin.php provides oversight of sitters, parents, jobs, and payout requests.

Notifications | Expo push notifications for job offers, status changes, chat messages, payout updates

Session persistence | expo-file-system session file restores active jobs after full app restart
